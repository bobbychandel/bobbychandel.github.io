Real-time problem solving

Plants don’t fail in textbooks. They fail at 2 a.m. Here are the kinds of problems I work through on site, each with a method.

Biogas quality changes day to day

Feed mix, temperature and digester load change methane and H₂S. Fix: continuous gas analysers, a buffer holder, and a pre-treatment stage (H₂S removal and dehumidification) sized for the worst day, not the average one.

Methane slip is eating revenue

Every 1% of methane lost to the off-gas is 1% of sellable gas gone. Fix: compare slip across technologies, add multi-stage membranes or off-gas recycle, and treat tail gas where required.

Compressor trips and moisture damage

Wet gas corrodes and fouls downstream equipment. Fix: chilling and dryer sizing from real dew-point data, plus coalescing filters before every compressor.

Site and land decisions

Plant layout, feedstock routes, gas-grid distance and safety zones all start with a survey. I take part in land surveys, such as the Hingonia CBG plant, so the design starts from facts on the ground.

Why engineering matters in CBG, Bio-CNG and refining

Safety

Biogas holds methane and toxic H₂S. Correct materials, zoning, relief and interlocks protect people first.

Gas quality

Buyers need CBG with high methane, low CO₂, low H₂S and low moisture. Engineering is how you prove it.

Money

Power use, methane slip and uptime decide if a plant earns or bleeds. Small design choices move IRR.

Environment

Good design captures methane that would otherwise escape and returns nutrient-rich fermented manure to farms.

Expertise: CBG and Bio-CNG refining systems

Raw biogas is about 50–60% methane, with CO₂, H₂S and water. Refining lifts methane to pipeline grade. Pick a technology to see how it works and where it fits.

Deep knowledge, so nobody can fool you about engineering

Know these questions and you can read any vendor offer.

“Zero methane loss”

Ask for guaranteed slip in %, with the test method and the tail-gas plan. No process is zero.

“Low power use”

Ask for kWh per Nm³ of raw gas, including compressors, chillers, heat and vacuum, not just the main unit.

“Same output as 10 TPD”

Check the feed gas figures. A plant is rated at a methane % and flow; change those and the rating changes.

“No H₂S problem”

Ask what removes H₂S, how often media changes, and what the cost per month is.
