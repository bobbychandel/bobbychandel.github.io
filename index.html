Real-time problem solving

Plants don’t fail in textbooks. They fail at 2 a.m. Here are the kinds of problems I work through on site, each with a method.

Biogas quality changes day to day

Feed mix, temperature and digester load change methane and H₂S. Fix: continuous gas analysers, a buffer holder, and a pre-treatment stage (H₂S removal and dehumidification) sized for the worst day, not the average one.

Methane slip is eating revenue

Every 1% of methane lost to the off-gas is 1% of sellable gas gone. Fix: compare slip across technologies, add multi-stage membranes or off-gas recycle, and treat tail gas where required.

Compressor trips and moisture damage

Wet gas corrodes and fouls downstream equipment. Fix: chilling and dryer sizing from real dew-point data, plus coalescing filters before every compressor.

Site and land decisions

Plant layout, feedstock routes, gas-grid distance and safety zones all start with a survey. I take part in land surveys, such as the Hingonia CBG plant, so the design starts from facts on the ground.
